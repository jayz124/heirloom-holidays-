About this preview

This is the front end of Heirloom Insider, built ahead of our connection to the Little Emperors API so that the whole member journey can be walked through and discussed.

What you are looking at

Hotels and photographs
Real hotels, shown with the hotels' own or Little Emperors photography.
Rates, rooms and privileges
Sample figures written for layout. Nothing here comes from the Little Emperors API, and no live rate or benefit is displayed.
Sign-in, payment and booking
Simulated in the browser. No account is created, no card is taken and nothing is sent to a hotel.

How the live version is built

A closed, identifiable membership
Every member has their own account and signs in with a one-time email link. Member pages are served only to a signed-in member.
A paid membership
£299 a year, or £30 a month, taken through Stripe. An account without an active membership cannot reach search, rates or booking.
Nothing public
Rates, privileges and availability are requested by our server after sign-in and are never part of a public page. The public Insider page names a handful of hotels and shows no rate or benefit. Member pages are kept out of search engines.
The API key
Held on the server only. It never reaches the browser.
Credit
"Powered by Little Emperors" sits in the footer of every Insider page.
Terms
Members accept the Little Emperors terms and privacy policy alongside our own when they join.

Walk through it

Start on the public page, join, then search, open a hotel, book a room and find it under My trips. Signing out returns you to the public page, and the member pages close again.
