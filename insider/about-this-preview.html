About this beta

Heirloom Insider is being built. This beta lets you search the hotels and ask for rates today, while live pricing is connected.

What you see today

Hotels and photographs
Real hotels, shown with the hotels' own or Little Emperors photography.
Rates
On request. Ask from any hotel and Kara replies with the member rate and the privileges for your dates.
Signed-in preview
Partners and invited guests can sign in to walk through the full journey: rates, rooms, booking and My trips. Every figure there is a sample written for layout, not a live price, and no booking is sent to a hotel.

How the live version is built

A closed, identifiable membership
Every member has their own account. Member pages are served only to a signed-in member.
A paid membership
Taken through Stripe. An account without an active membership cannot reach rates or booking.
Nothing public
Rates, privileges and availability are requested by our server after sign-in and are never part of a public page.
The API key
Held on the server only. It never reaches the browser.
Credit
"Powered by Little Emperors" in the footer of every member page.
