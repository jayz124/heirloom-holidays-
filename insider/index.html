Questions

Why is there a membership fee?

The rates and privileges inside Insider are private. Our hotel partners allow them to be shown only to a closed group of members, never on a public website. Membership is what makes you part of that group.

Where do the rates come from?

Heirloom Holidays is a member of Little Emperors, a private network of more than 5,000 luxury hotels. Insider connects you to the same rates and privileges Kara uses for her own clients.

Can Kara still plan my whole trip?

Yes. Bespoke planning carries on exactly as before, and as a member you can hand any Insider booking to Kara to build the rest of the trip around it.

What happens after I book?

You receive a confirmation with your booking reference, and the stay appears under My trips. The hotel sees your privileges on the booking, so there is nothing to ask for when you arrive.
